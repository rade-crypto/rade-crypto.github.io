# Daniel's court-story details that also occur in earlier biblical books (BHSA lexeme search)

A detail found in an older biblical book was available to any later Jewish writer, so it cannot show 6th-century knowledge.

| Daniel | Detail | Elsewhere in the Hebrew Bible |
|---|---|---|
| 2:14, 15, 24, 25 | Arioch (אריוך), the king's officer | only Genesis 14:1, 9 (Arioch king of Ellasar, in Abraham's time) |
| 2:14 | "chief of the guard/executioners" (רב טבחיא) | רב טבחים: 2 Kings 25:8-20; Jeremiah 39-52 (Nebuzaradan); Genesis 37:36-41:12 (Potiphar) |
| 2:1, 3 | "his spirit was troubled" (ותתפעם רוחו), rare verb פעם | Genesis 41:8 (Pharaoh's dream), Judges 13:25, Psalm 77:5 |
| 1:20; 2:2, 10, 27; 4:4, 6; 5:11 | "magicians" (חרטמים, an Egyptian loanword) | Genesis 41:8, 24; Exodus 7-9 (Pharaoh's magicians) |
| 1:5 | daily royal allowance "day by day" (דבר יום ביומו) | 2 Kings 25:30 (Jehoiachin at the Babylonian court) |
| 1:2 | "the land of Shinar" | Genesis 10:10; 11:2; 14:1, 9; Isaiah 11:11; Zechariah 5:11 |
| 2:5; 3:29 | houses "made a dunghill" (נולי/נולו) | only Ezra 6:11 (decree of Darius) |
| 6:9, 13, 16 | a royal law that cannot be revoked | Esther 1:19; 8:8 |
| 6:18 | decree sealed with the royal signet | Esther 3:10-12; 8:8, 10 |
| 1:3 | "chief official" (רב סריסים) | Jeremiah 39:3, 13; 2 Kings 18:17 |
| 2:1-49 | foreign youth interprets the king's dream after the court experts fail | Genesis 41 (Joseph) |
