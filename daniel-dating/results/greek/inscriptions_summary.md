# Greek inscriptions: συμφωνία, ψαλτήριον, κιθαρ-

Source: I.PHI (Sommerschield, Assael et al. 2021; cleaned subset of the Packard Humanities Institute *Searchable Greek Inscriptions*), Hugging Face `GBaker/I.PHI`, 88,202 inscriptions, about 3.9 million words, 53,220 with editors' date ranges. Searched after stripping accents (script: `scripts/greek/search_inscriptions.py`; all hits: `inscriptions_hits.csv`).

## συμφων- (39 hits)
Noun συμφωνία, every dated occurrence:
| PHI id | place | date | context | sense |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 77222 | Cyclades (IG XII Suppl. p. 96) | 257 BCE | "renew the friendship and the *concord* (συμφωνίαν) with the Rhodians" | agreement |
| 77221 | Cyclades (IG XII,5 Add. p. 303) | (same formula, later copy) | "the friendship and the concord with the Rhodians" | agreement |
| 184769 | Black Sea | Roman imperial (27 BCE-284 CE) | fragmentary, beside χρησμῷ "oracle" | unclear |
| 278836, 278837 | Bithynia | 122-131 and 184/5 CE | villages' festival contributions listed with "wine-drinking," "gymnasiarchy," "lamp-lighting": "συμφωνίαν" | a music band (service provided) |
| 148571 | Thessaly | undated epitaph | "the stele reveals the *harmony* (συμφωνίην)..." | harmony |
| 177747 | Italy | undated epigram | "the Muse... the lovely pēktis, *symphonia* mixed with lovely songs" | musical harmony |

All other hits are the adjective σύμφωνος ("in agreement") or the verb συμφωνέω ("agree"), from the mid-3rd century BCE on, in treaties, boundary decisions and contracts. **No inscription uses συμφωνία for a musical instrument.** The earliest concrete musical sense in inscriptions is the 2nd-century CE "band."

## ψαλτήριον (1 hit)
PHI 63877, Delos temple inventory, 146/5-145/4 BCE: "golden and silver eyes, a dedication... beside the *psaltērion* (πρὸς τῶι ψαλτηρίωι)." Related: ψάλται (harpists) at Delos, 236 BCE (PHI 62582); χοροψάλτρια at Delphi, 134 and 86 BCE.

## κιθαρ- (296 hits)
κιθαρῳδός, κιθαριστής in Athenian prize lists from 402/1 BCE (PHI 290960) and Eretria after 340 BCE (PHI 80770). Too early to bear on dating.

## Combined with the literary search (Perseus + First1K, 36M words) and papyri.info (5M words)
- συμφωνία: c. 400 BCE onward only as consonance/harmony/agreement; first concrete musical use in Polybius (mid-2nd c. BCE) about Antiochus IV and the games of 167 BCE ("band" or "instrument"); as a named instrument in a list only in Greek Daniel; papyri use it for a troupe of musicians from 181 CE.
- ψαλτήριον: Theophrastus c. 300 BCE; Septuagint Genesis (3rd c. BCE); Delos inventory 146/5 BCE.
- Not searched: the TLG (subscription only) and the full PHI (blocks automated access); I.PHI is a cleaned subset of PHI.
