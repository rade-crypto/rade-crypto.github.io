# Daniel 7 vs Daniel 2-6: correction

An earlier version of this file reported that chapter 7 uses later Aramaic forms more often than chapters 2-6
(43% vs 16%, p = 0.006). That result does not survive the comparison with the Persian-era papyri:

- It pooled the reflexive prefix (ht- vs ʾt-). The 5th-century Elephantine papyri already write ʾt- in 31 of 32 cases,
  so this prefix is not a time marker (Biblical Aramaic's ht- is a convention of its own).
- It counted causative imperfects without a written h- as later forms; counting only forms in which the prefix is always
  written, chapter 7 has 9 of 9 old (h-) forms.

With only valid features (3rd pl. pronoun, plural demonstrative, causative prefix in perfect-type forms), chapter 7 has
2 later forms out of 11 (the pronoun ʾnyn and the demonstrative ʾlyn) and chapters 2-6 have 9 later forms out of 106 (Fisher p = 0.28).
There are too few tokens to tell the chapters apart statistically. The claim is withdrawn.
