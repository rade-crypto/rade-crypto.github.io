# Two layers in Daniel's Aramaic: stories (chs. 2-6) vs vision (ch. 7)

Pooled over the four morphological features that change between Achaemenid and Hellenistic Aramaic
(3rd pl. pronoun ʾnwn, plural demonstrative ʾlyn, ʾt- reflexive prefix, ʾ- causative prefix),
counted in the consonantal Masoretic text (data: aramaic_features.csv).

| text | later forms | total | share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ezra 4:8-6:18, 7:12-26 | 6 | 55 | 11% |
| Daniel 2 | 8 | 53 | 15% |
| Daniel 3 | 3 | 30 | 10% |
| Daniel 4 | 4 | 12 | 33% |
| Daniel 5 | 4 | 33 | 12% |
| Daniel 6 | 7 | 39 | 18% |
| **Daniel 2-6 together** | 26 | 167 | **16%** |
| **Daniel 7** | 9 | 21 | **43%** |
| Qumran Aramaic (3rd-1st c. BCE) | 621 | 734 | 85% |

Fisher exact tests: Daniel 7 vs Daniel 2-6, odds ratio 4.1, p = 0.006.
Daniel 2-6 vs Ezra, odds ratio 1.5, p = 0.51 (no detectable difference on these four features).
On the 3rd/2nd pl. suffixes (-hwn, -kwn) all of Daniel 2-7 is already 100% later form, against Ezra's 58% / 17%.

Reading: the court stories share the morphology of Ezra's Persian-era Aramaic apart from the suffixes; the vision of
chapter 7 is measurably later, between the stories and Qumran.
