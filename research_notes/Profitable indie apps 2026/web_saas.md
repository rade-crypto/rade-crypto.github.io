# Profitable Indie Web Apps / Micro-SaaS / AI Tools for Solo Founders & Tiny Teams (2025 – Oct 2026)

Research method note: WebFetch failed (DNS errors) for every page tried (trustmrr.com, medium.com, bigideasdb.com), so these notes rest on search-engine result summaries of the cited pages, not on full reads. Founder X posts were seen only through result snippets. Treat every figure as self-reported or third-party-estimated unless marked otherwise. **Survivorship bias warning:** every source below shows winners. Founders who post revenue publicly (Levels, Marc Lou) earn part of their income *from* the audience that the posting builds. Listicle and aggregator sites (flowjam, creem, superframeworks, ideaproof, questera, profitable.app) often copy each other and mix data from different years.

## Q1: Which categories are working now for indie SaaS?

### Takeaway
The best-documented indie earners in 2025–2026 are: (a) vertical consumer AI image/photo tools such as AI headshots, AI photo and interior design, now past their peak; (b) "chat UI / AI client" products such as TypingMind; (c) tools sold to other founders (analytics, revenue verification, SEO/content, Reddit marketing, boilerplates and courses); (d) AI content and SEO tools such as Outrank; (e) forms, testimonials and other narrow utility SaaS (Tally, Senja); and (f) Chrome extensions built on top of big platforms (ChatGPT, LinkedIn). Consumer AI subscription apps with paid social are producing the largest new numbers, but many of them are mobile (e.g., Stella).

### Inferences
- Categories with documented indie revenue in 2026: AI photo and imaging (declining from peak), AI chat clients sold to teams, founder tools (analytics, revenue verification, SEO, Reddit marketing), AI SEO writers, forms and testimonials, and Chrome extensions on top of big platforms. Products sold to other founders do well when the founder already has an audience.
- Boilerplates and courses look like a fading category. ShipFast fell from $17.2K (Jan) to roughly $2–3.5K (Jun–Sep 2026) and CodeFast fell from $23.5K to $7K. AI coding agents that generate scaffolding probably erode the boilerplate value proposition (inference).
- Single-model "AI photo" tools appear to decay over 1–3 years as models commoditize and competitors such as BetterPic and Aragon flood in. The founders who win early tend to sell (Postma/HeadshotPro) or diversify (Levels).

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 data found for AI video clipping (e.g., Crayo), AI interior-design competitors, AI resume tools other than Rezi's snapshot, transcription tools, or directories as businesses.
- No verified data on niche AI agents for vertical workflows; there are only blog claims.
- The TrustMRR leaderboard could not be read directly (fetch failed), so I could not compile a ranked list of verified solo products.

## Q2: Concrete examples, what's current in 2026 vs. what's faded

### Takeaway
Current: Marc Lou's TrustMRR (~$30–53K/mo) and DataFast (~$21–31K/mo); TypingMind (claimed six figures/mo); Levels' total portfolio (~$189K–$250K/mo of business revenue). Faded: ShipFast (from $17K/mo in Jan 2026 to ~$2–3.5K/mo), CodeFast, and Photo AI (from a ~$132–138K peak to ~$80K/mo by one account). Interior AI has slipped. HeadshotPro was reportedly sold.

### Cited Findings
- Levels (Sep 23, 2026): business revenue plus investment gains passed $10M/yr at a ~94.5% profit margin. Investment returns have exceeded business revenue for about a year, and most gains are unrealised — [levels.io](https://levels.io/passed-10m-revenue-investment-gains); [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/note/pieter-levels-passes-10m-yr-in-revenue-and-investment-gains). Dealroom says business revenue is ~$200–250K/mo — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/note/pieter-levels-passes-10m-yr-in-revenue-and-investment-gains)
- Levels' X/creator revenue hit ~$25K in Aug 2026 (X payouts alone ~$19,312 on ~73M views) — [levels.io](https://levels.io/x-revenue-new-record-this-month)
- His disclosed product streams total ~$189K/mo, a "portfolio reset rather than collapse" — [StealWhatWorks](https://stealwhatworks.com/blogs/news/pieter-levels-still-making-money)
- RemoteOK: $492K/yr (~$41K/mo), self-reported according to an aggregator — [profitable.app](https://profitable.app/people/pieter-levels)
- fly.pieter.com, a vibe-coded game: reportedly hit a $1M/yr run rate in 17 days, then "went to zero" (title of a DEV post) — [DEV](https://dev.to/promptway/he-built-a-flight-simulator-in-three-hours-and-hit-1m-a-year-in-17-days-then-it-went-to-zero-1b1l)
- ShipFast decline (Marc's reported figures): $17.2K Jan, $9.2K Mar, $6.2K Apr, $3K Jun, $4K Jul 2026 — [Streakr](https://streakr.co/playbook/marc-lou). ShipFast had made $528K in its first 4 months (older Starter Story data, 2023–24) — [Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/marc-lou-shipfast)
- Kleo (LinkedIn content Chrome extension): $0 to $62K MRR in 3 months (Medium roundup, low reliability) — [Medium](https://medium.com/@tamimbuilds/8-solo-founders-who-quietly-hit-20k-62k-mrr-in-the-last-6-months-5032e610badc)

### Inferences
- Pattern: revenue in a given product decays as models commoditize. The durable asset is the founder's audience, which lets them launch the next product fast. Levels and Marc Lou both now earn materially from audience/creator revenue and from meta-products about indie revenue.
- Time to revenue for audience-backed founders is days to weeks (Photo AI reached $40K MRR in ~60 days; fly.pieter.com had a $1M run rate in 17 days). Founders without an audience are not comparable.

### Gaps
- No primary 2026 disclosure for HeadshotPro's sale terms, TypingMind's 2026 revenue, or Photo AI's exact current MRR.

## Q3: How have Google AI Overviews / AI search changed SEO-led growth, and what channels work now?

### Takeaway
Organic CTR on queries that show AI Overviews dropped sharply between 2024 and 2025. Seer found 1.76% falling to 0.61%, with a partial rebound reported by Feb 2026. Google's March 2026 scaled-content-abuse enforcement hit thin programmatic SEO pages. AI-referred traffic (87% of it from ChatGPT) converts much better but is still small in volume. The channels that work for first customers are borrowed communities (Reddit, niche groups), owned audiences (X/build-in-public), and marketplace search (Chrome Web Store). Getting cited in AI answers (GEO) is the new SEO goal.

### Cited Findings
- Seer Interactive: organic CTR on AIO queries fell from 1.76% to 0.61% (June 2024 to Sept 2025) across 2.43B impressions, 53 brands and 5.47M queries. CTR reportedly rebounded to 2.4% by Feb 2026, but a ~37% gap vs non-AIO queries remains — [quickseo.ai](https://quickseo.ai/blog/google-ai-overviews-statistics-2026-60-data-points-every-seo-should-know); [omnibound](https://www.omnibound.ai/blog/google-ai-overviews-statistics)
- Pew (July 2025): CTR is 8% with an AI Overview present vs 15% without — [omnibound](https://www.omnibound.ai/blog/ai-seo-statistics)
- Smaller sites lost 60% of search referrals in two years vs 22% for large publishers (publisher dataset) — [xseek](https://www.xseek.io/blogs/articles/ai-traffic-decline-2026)
- Brands cited in AIOs earn 35% more organic clicks and 91% more paid clicks (ALM Corp 2026). Non-cited brands saw CTR decline 67% over 2025 — [visible-authority](https://visible-authority.com/blog/how-google-ai-overviews-change-organic-traffic-2026)
- ChatGPT drives 87.4% of AI referral traffic. AI-referred visitors are reported to convert at 14.2% vs 2.8% for Google organic; another source says 15.9% vs 1.76%. Even so, ChatGPT referrals are under 1% of all referrals to publishers — [pixis](https://pixis.ai/blog/why-ai-search-traffic-converts-at-4-5x-what-the-data-actually-shows); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/terdawn-deboe/2026/05/18/google-ai-overviews-are-eating-your-website-traffic-fight-back/)
- GA4 under-attributes AI-influenced traffic: a visitor who finds a brand in ChatGPT and then searches its name shows up as branded organic — [pixis](https://pixis.ai/blog/why-ai-search-traffic-converts-at-4-5x-what-the-data-actually-shows)
- Programmatic SEO is "not dead, but the low-value version is". Google's March 2026 scaled-content-abuse enforcement hit thin pSEO sites, and AIOs cut top-result clicks by 58%, hitting definition-style pages hardest — [madx.digital](https://www.madx.digital/learn/programmatic-seo-ai-overviews)
- First-customer dataset of 70 solo founders (2024–2026, author unidentified): borrowed communities 34% of named channels (Reddit 15 cases), owned audience 20%, cold outreach 15% — [lead-scorer](https://lead-scorer.com/blog/how-founders-get-first-customers-2026)
- Niche-community launches reportedly had 3.4x higher week-1 paid conversion than Product Hunt launches (vendor claim, no methodology) — [questera](https://www.questera.ai/blogs/micro-saas-ideas-indie-hackers-ai)
- Paid acquisition at small scale: an indie hacker reached $6,772 MRR on $20/day of Meta ads — [MRRStory](https://www.mrrstory.com/stories/this-indie-hacker-reached-6772-mrr-with-just-20day-meta-ads)
- Affiliates: HeadshotPro runs a 30% recurring affiliate program — [revshare.so](https://www.revshare.so/programs/advertiser/headshotpro)
- Stripe 2025 annual letter: 20% of Atlas startups charged their first customer within 30 days in 2025 (vs 8% in 2020). The number of companies reaching $10M ARR within 3 months of launch doubled vs 2024 — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-2025-update)

### Inferences
- SEO-first indie playbooks (Postma-style SEO, Levels' early sites) now carry more risk for informational keywords. They still work for commercial/tool-intent queries ("AI headshot generator") and for data-rich pages that AIs cite.
- Build-in-public on X looks like the strongest documented channel for the top earners, and it also pays directly through X creator revenue.
- Time to first dollar is falling (Stripe data), which matches the "ship fast, test many" pattern.

### Gaps
- No independent, verified indie-specific dataset on GEO/AI-citation revenue impact; all of it is vendor case studies.
- No 2026 data found on YouTube-led or Product Hunt-led indie revenue outcomes.

## Q4: What's saturated or dying, and what's the moat pattern for survivors?

### Takeaway
Thin generic ChatGPT wrappers (writing, chat, generic summarizers) are being absorbed by ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini features, and falling model prices squeeze their margins. Boilerplates and first-wave AI photo apps show revenue decay. The moats survivors share are distribution (an owned audience, SEO/brand, affiliates), proprietary data or feedback loops, workflow lock-in with team/B2B features (TypingMind Team), and narrow vertical focus where the buyer base is large enough.

### Cited Findings
- Jasper's revenue fell after ChatGPT launched, as customers swapped $50/mo plans for free or $20 ChatGPT plans (Sacra) — [DEV](https://dev.to/jamilxt/ai-wrappers-are-dying-three-business-models-survived-instead-5db4); [aieatingtheworld](https://www.aieatingtheworld.com/articles/ai-wrapper-business-why-most-fail-2026)
- Foundation-model pricing reportedly collapsed 98% within a year, shrinking the wrapper margin layer — [tianpan.co](https://tianpan.co/blog/2026/04/12/the-ai-wrapper-trap-when-your-moat-is-someone-elses-api-call)
- Widely circulated failure statistics ("80% fail", "~3% survive, per McKinsey") have no locatable primary report — [aieatingtheworld](https://www.aieatingtheworld.com/articles/ai-wrapper-business-why-most-fail-2026)
- Decision rule from these sources: build a wrapper only if it owns proprietary data that compounds, workflow lock-in, or a distribution channel horizontal tools can't reach. Vertical pivots fail when the buyer base is too small (e.g., "AI SDR for legal") — [preuve.ai](https://preuve.ai/blog/are-ai-wrapper-startups-worth-building-2026); [hatchworks](https://hatchworks.com/blog/gen-ai/ai-wrapper-product-strategy/)
- A Google VP flagged sustainability concerns about two AI startup categories, wrappers and aggregators — [storyboard18](https://storyboard18.com/brand-makers/no-patience-for-that-anymore-google-vp-flags-sustainability-concerns-for-two-ai-startup-categories-90541.htm)
- Solo founders reportedly stall around $50–150K MRR when the operational load exceeds one person plus agents (blog claim) — [taskade](https://www.taskade.com/blog/one-person-companies)
- Photo AI's revenue fell while traffic estimates rose, which points to conversion and retention pressure in a commoditizing category — [StealWhatWorks](https://stealwhatworks.com/blogs/news/pieter-levels-still-making-money)

### Inferences
- Survivors convert a consumer AI tool into B2B or team seats (TypingMind), stack many small products on one audience (Marc Lou, Levels), or sell out near the peak (HeadshotPro).
- Saturated: generic AI writers, generic chat-with-PDF, AI headshots (many clones such as BetterPic and Aragon), boilerplates, and ChatGPT-enhancer extensions at risk from native features.

### Gaps
- No hard churn or retention data for indie AI tools in 2026. ChartMogul and Baremetrics 2026 benchmark reports were not retrieved.

## Q5: Chrome extensions and other marketplaces (Chrome Web Store, Figma, WordPress, Notion, Slack)

### Takeaway
Chrome extensions can reach 5-figure MRR solo, usually by riding a big platform (ChatGPT, LinkedIn) and using Chrome Web Store search for distribution. The median outcome is tiny. Figma plugins and Notion templates are mostly side income ($200–$2.5K/mo typical). No 2025–2026 data was found for WordPress or Slack.

### Cited Findings
- Superpower ChatGPT: a free extension built in days, now at 5-figure MRR and still run solo (founder on Indie Hackers) — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/building-a-free-chrome-extension-in-3-days-and-turning-it-into-a-5-figure-mrr-ecosystem-3rIbjigZxiFsrgqJjJYp)
- A solo developer's extension at ~$10K MRR: 8% free-to-paid conversion, priced per 1,000 actions, distribution entirely from Chrome Web Store search — [iHeart podcast](https://www.iheart.com/podcast/53-the-indie-hacker-podcast-wi-334456085/episode/how-a-solo-dev-hit-10k-338614911/)
- Counterexample: TimeYourWeb has 20,000 users, 10 paying customers and $60 MRR after 10 years — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-built-a-chrome-extension-in-2-weeks-10-years-later-20-000-users-10-paying-customers-60-mrr-YaK5THoaKqXgTlgBjHgO)
- Kleo (LinkedIn extension): $0 to $62K MRR in 3 months (low-reliability roundup) — [Medium](https://medium.com/@tamimbuilds/8-solo-founders-who-quietly-hit-20k-62k-mrr-in-the-last-6-months-5032e610badc)
- Extension acquisitions are valued at 24–40x MRR — [exitbid](https://exitbid.io/blog/sell-chrome-extension). Manifest V3 forbids loading remote JS — [chromegoldmine](https://chromegoldmine.com/blog/how-to-build-chrome-extensions/)
- Figma plugins: a claim that 87 plugins earned over $1K/mo in 2026 per Figma payout data (unverified). The median for paid plugins with 500+ installs is $200–800/mo — [jakeinsight](https://jakeinsight.com/side-income/2026-03-12-create-and-sell-figma-plugins-as-a-developer/)
- Notion templates: niche sellers report $800–2,500/mo after 6–9 months. The typical 12-month total is $4K–18K — [kupkaike](https://kupkaike.com/blog/is-selling-notion-templates-still-profitable-2026); [coachli](https://www.coachli.co/blog/sell-notion-templates-your-2026-side-hustle-blueprint)
- Notionlytics (Notion analytics add-on): $43,160 MRR (aggregator, date unclear) — [bigideasdb](https://bigideasdb.com/solo-developer-saas-monthly-revenue-examples)

### Inferences
- Marketplace search (the Chrome Web Store) is one of the few remaining "free distribution" channels. It carries platform risk: the host can add the feature natively, as with ChatGPT enhancers.
- Add-ons to big platforms (a Notion analytics tool, a LinkedIn writing tool) do better than templates.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 revenue data found for WordPress plugins, Slack apps, Shopify apps or Zapier integrations.
- No aggregate Chrome Web Store revenue distribution data.
