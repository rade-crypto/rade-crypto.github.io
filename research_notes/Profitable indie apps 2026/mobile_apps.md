## Q1. What do RevenueCat / Appfigures / Sensor Tower say about which categories earn most and convert best?

### Takeaway
RevenueCat's 2026 report (2025 data, about 115k apps, about $16B revenue) shows revenue piling up at the top: the top 10% of apps grew MRR 306% YoY while the bottom quartile fell 33%. AI apps convert to paid better (8.5% vs 5.6%) but keep fewer users (21% annual retention vs 31%). Proven categories for indies include AI calorie tracking (Cal AI), looksmaxxing/self-improvement (Umax), faith (Bible Chat, Hallow), AI photo, astrology/fortune-telling and AI companions.

### Cited Findings
- RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026 covers more than 115,000 apps and more than $16B in revenue, using 2025 data. It is a 339-page report in two parts, with 11 category breakouts inside the PDF. — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps); [apfelpatient summary](https://www.apfelpatient.de/en/news/subscription-apps-why-only-a-few-apps-are-growing-strongly)
- Top 25% of apps grew MRR 80% YoY, top 10% grew 306%, and the bottom quartile fell 33% ("the app middle class is dying"). — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/the-app-middle-class-is-dying-and-revenuecats-data-shows-exactly-how-fast/); [Subscription Insider](https://www.subscriptioninsider.com/blog/revenuecat-data-shows-subscription-app-growth-concentrating-at-the-top-news)
- AI apps: trial-to-paid 8.5% vs 5.6% for non-AI. Annual retention 21.1% vs 30.7%. Median refund rate 4.2% vs 3.5%. Revenue per user about 41% higher, churn about 30% faster. — [TechCrunch, Mar 10 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/10/ai-powered-apps-struggle-with-long-term-retention-new-report-shows); [xix.ai](https://xix.ai/ainews/revenuecat-2026-report-ai-app-retention-rate-at-21-churn-30-faster-than-traditional-apps.html); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/ai-apps-earn-41-more-per-user-but-churn-30-faster-revenuecat-finds/)
- Median trial-to-paid conversion reported as about 53%, up from about 50% in 2024, partly due to survivorship (secondary source, not verified against RevenueCat). This is a different metric from the 8.5%/5.6% above, which may be measured from download or install. — [PPC Land via search summary](https://ppc.land/the-app-middle-class-is-dying-and-revenuecats-data-shows-exactly-how-fast/)
- Weekly plans convert installs to trials at about 9.8%, vs about 1.8% for annual plans. Trials of 17–32 days convert at a 42.5% median, vs 25.5% for trials of 4 days or less. In Health & Fitness, trials lift LTV by 63.6%. (Third-party reading of RevenueCat and Adapty data.) — [Medium, Chuvak Pavel](https://chuvak-pavel.medium.com/state-of-app-monetization-2026-key-trends-from-revenuecat-and-adapty-8bdc23a4bb6f)
- Hard paywall revenue per install of $3.09 vs $0.38 for freemium/trial at Day 60 (unverified third-party blog). — [buildmvpfast](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/blog/hard-paywall-vs-free-trial-revenuecat-indie-app-2026)
- Appfigures, April 2026: TikTok #1 by revenue ($303M net), ChatGPT #2 ($211M), Claude at #9 in global downloads (20M). Top 10 apps earned about $1.2–1.3B/month net in Jan–Feb 2026, dominated by big companies. — [Appfigures Apr 2026](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/most-downloaded-highest-earning-apps-april-2026); [Jan 2026](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/most-downloaded-highest-earning-apps-january-2026); [Feb 2026](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/most-downloaded-highest-earning-apps-february-2026)
- Appfigures: AI app revenue reached $1.8B all-time by 2024 (older data). — [Appfigures AI report](https://resources-cdn.appfigures.com/industry-reports/appfigures-report-rise-of-ai-apps-key-trends-shaping-2025.pdf)
- AI companion apps: $82M consumer spend in H1 2025, on track for more than $120M in 2025 (Appfigures via TechCrunch). — [TechBuzz](https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/breaking-ai-companion-apps-hit-120m-revenue-run-rate)
- Faith apps: Appfigures estimates Hallow at about $40M net revenue in 2025, peaking around Lent ($9.7M in April 2025), with a "flat" trajectory. Bible Chat had $6.3M net lifetime by April 2025, more than 95% from the US, best month $750K. Bible Chat itself claimed 10M users and $15M annualized revenue in Feb 2025 ($14M Series A, Romanian team). — [Appfigures Hallow](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/hallow-lent-surge-prayer-app-revenue); [Appfigures Bible](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20250418/amp?f=5); [Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/bible-chat-investment-round-faith-app-romania-feb-2025)
- Bible Chat was described as #1 in Reference, with 1M monthly downloads and $250K MRR (X post, Mar 2025, self-promotional thread). — [Steven Cravotta on X](https://x.com/StevenCravotta/status/1899897706312577285)

### Inferences
- The categories that work for indies combine a visual "magic moment" from AI (scan food, rate face, transform photo, answer from scripture) with an easy demo for short video and a recurring self-improvement or identity need. Health/fitness, self-improvement, faith, astrology and AI photo all fit.
- AI apps' high conversion and weak retention push monetization toward annual plans or hard paywalls with weekly options, so revenue arrives before churn.
- A tiny Eastern-European team (Bible Chat, Romania) reaching about $15M ARR mostly from the US is a direct analogue for a Serbian developer.

## Q2. Concrete recent indie success stories with numbers, timelines and team sizes

### Takeaway
Cal AI is the clearest recent case: teen founders, more than $30M annual revenue and 15M+ downloads in under two years, then acquired by MyFitnessPal (closed Dec 2025, announced Mar 2026) with a 7-person team. Blake Anderson ran the same playbook three times (RizzGPT, Umax, Cal AI). TrustMRR shows verified indie apps in the $20k–$65k MRR band in AI photo, astrology, makeup and calorie tracking.

### Cited Findings
- **Cal AI** (Zach Yadegari, Henry Langmack, Blake Anderson): MyFitnessPal acquired it after almost a year of talks. The deal closed Dec 2025 and was announced Mar 2026. MyFitnessPal cites more than 15M downloads and more than $30M annual revenue in under two years. The 7 employees plus contractors were retained, and the app stays standalone. Price undisclosed (rumoured above $100M, unconfirmed). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3098039); [Athletech News](https://athletechnews.com/myfitnesspal-cal-ai-acquisition); [Appetizer Mobile](https://appetizermobile.com/2026/03/02/myfitnesspal-acquires-cal-ai/)
- Other Cal AI figures conflict: "more than $40M sales in the last 12 months" — [Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/note/myfitnesspal-acquires-teen-built-cal-ai-after-40m-in-annual-sales); "$50M+ ARR in 18 months" — [Superframeworks](https://superframeworks.com/case-study/cal-ai); "$50M ARR" — [Tiffany Zhong on X](https://x.com/tzhongg/status/2028931446317232397?lang=en). Treat $30M+ as the company-confirmed figure.
- Yadegari then raised $10M for a new startup, Persona (led by Vine Ventures). — [Ascendants](https://ascendants.in/founders-corner/zach-yadegari-cal-ai-founder-success-story-persona-ai-startup/)
- **Blake Anderson**: In Oct 2024 he posted "RizzGPT ($2.4m arr), Umax ($5m arr), and Cal AI ($8m arr)… idea to execution 3x on this playbook in one year."   - Boothie (AI photo editor): $44,405 over the last 30 days, about $51.8k MRR. iOS launch June 2026, Android Sept 2026, so about 4 months to $50k MRR. Listed for sale at $1.5M. — [TrustMRR Boothie](https://trustmrr.com/startup/boothie)

## Q3. Which growth playbooks work in 2026, what they cost, and what's saturating

### Takeaway
Organic short video (faceless slideshows, screen-recording demos, multi-account TikTok/IG networks, paid UGC creators) remains the main indie channel. Apple Search Ads (about $1–2.50 per US tap) captures intent. Signs of saturation: post volume on TikTok is up sharply while engagement per post is falling.

### Cited Findings
- Cal AI's early growth is attributed to simple screen-recording explainer videos with voiceover. The vendor-blog claim of "$1.5M MRR in 34 days" is called exceptional and is unverified. — [Vidotoria / faceless-content blog](https://www.vidotoria.com/blog/how-to-market-app-without-showing-your-face)
- Faceless app account formats: screen recordings, photo carousels, AI creator photos, text-over-stock, comparison slides. — [ReelCarousel](https://www.reelcarousel.app/blog/faceless-tiktok-accounts-promoting-apps)
- Superwall's "how to design a viral app in 2025" series covers the paywall and virality design playbook. — [Superwall](https://superwall.com/blog/part-2-how-to-design-a-viral-app-in-2025)
- Human UGC creator pricing for 2026 is commonly $100–$500 per video, $200–$400 for an established creator including 90-day ad usage, and $500–$1,200 for professionals. Paid-ad usage rights add 25–100%. AI UGC tools run about $2–20 per video (vendor claim). — [Influee](https://influee.co/blog/ugc-price); [JoinBrands](https://joinbrands.com/blog/ugc-creator-rates/); [Sparkiz](https://sparkiz.ai/blog/ugc-ads-cost/)
- About 5% of Meta creatives become "winners" (about 20 tests per winner), so the real cost per winning creative is several thousand dollars at human-creator rates. — [Riffkit](https://riffkit.ai/blog/ugc-creator-rates-and-the-wrong-number)
- Apple Search Ads 2026: US cost per tap about $1.58, cost per acquisition about $2.51. UK $1.31 CPT / $2.02 CPA. Cheap markets as low as $0.11. Other datasets put US CPT at $1.56–$2.25. Subscription apps pay $1.00–$3.50 per tap. — [Adapty benchmarks 2026](https://adapty.io/blog/apple-ads-benchmarks-2026/); [Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/marketplace/apple-search-ads/research/apple-search-ads-costs/); [Intentplus](https://intentplus.io/blog/apple-ads-benchmarks-2026)
- Saturation signal: on TikTok, video post volume grew 72% and image/carousel post volume grew 140% YoY, while "almost every performance metric dropped." Branded engagement is expected to fall below 2% by mid-2026. — [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/tiktok-statistics-2026-marketing-data-points); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/tiktok-trends/)
- POV Camera case: slideshow content "does ok/good, but engagement is low," and was made to work through account volume. — [Social Growth Engineers](https://www.socialgrowthengineers.com/pov-camera-app-faceless-tiktok-strategy-to-100k-mrr-200k-dl)

### Inferences
- Carousel/slideshow formats are visibly crowding (140% volume growth). The edge is moving to more accounts, better hooks and niche angles rather than the format itself.
- A reasonable paid test budget for an indie: a few thousand dollars for Apple Search Ads plus about 10–20 creator videos ($2k–$8k). Judge it on cost per trial and per subscriber, not CPI.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2026 Meta or TikTok CPI benchmarks for subscription apps were found.
- No hard data on what creator-network operators (per-view pay schemes) charge.

## Q4. Typical costs: AI API per user, ad spend, creator payments, margins

### Takeaway
AI wrapper gross margins are commonly 25–60%, vs 80–90% for classic SaaS, unless pricing is set at 3.5–5x token cost. Store fees take another 15–30%. Light text or vision use per user (a few dollars a month) is cheap relative to $10–$70/year subscriptions, but power users and image or video generation can wipe out margin.

### Cited Findings
- AI wrapper gross margins typically run 25–60%. ICONIQ 2026 data puts the average AI product gross margin at 52%. Pricing at 3.5–5x token cost yields about 70–80% gross margin. — [Beancount](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/13/llm-api-cost-cogs-ai-wrapper-gross-margin-guide); [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/price-ai-wrapper); [mktclarity](https://mktclarity.com/blogs/news/margins-ai-wrapper)
- Worked example: 300 chat requests/month cost about $1.87 in API spend. Power users (10% of the base) costing $50/month each can make those users loss-making. — [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/ai-wrapper-business-model-monetization)
- One case study: a reported 91% gross margin was really 58% once inference was moved into COGS. — [Beancount](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/13/llm-api-cost-cogs-ai-wrapper-gross-margin-guide)
- AI apps also refund more: 4.2% median vs 3.5%. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/10/ai-powered-apps-struggle-with-long-term-retention-new-report-shows)
- Creator and ad costs: see Q3 (UGC $100–$500/video; Apple Search Ads about $1.58 US CPT).

### Inferences
- A single-scan vision call (like Cal AI) costs cents. At a typical $30–$70/year price, inference is a small share next to store fees and ad spend. For most indie consumer apps, marketing is the dominant cost, not AI.
- Image or video generation apps carry far heavier per-use costs. They need credit caps or weekly plans.

### Gaps
- No sourced per-image or per-video generation prices were retrieved.
- No published P&L from an indie consumer AI app (ad spend vs revenue) was found.

## Q5. Can a Serbian developer publish and get paid on the App Store and Google Play? Small Business Program 15%

### Takeaway
Very likely yes on both stores. Serbia has had an App Store storefront since April 2020, and Google added Serbia as a seller country years ago with RSD pricing. But no official Apple page explicitly confirming Serbian bank payouts was retrieved, and a 2026 Apple forum thread reports a Serbian individual unable to enroll, so this should be verified. Apple's Small Business Program gives 15% commission on under $1M proceeds in the prior calendar year. You must apply; it is not automatic.

### Cited Findings
- App Store services became available to users in Serbia on April 21, 2020 (one of 20 new countries). — [RATEL (Serbian regulator)](https://ratel.rs/en/blog/apple-services-available-to-users-in-serbia); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/apple/2020/app-store-rolling-out-to-20-countries/)
- An Apple Developer Forums thread (2026) from a Serbian developer reports an immediate error on "Enroll today" as an individual, the same result on two Apple Accounts, and no reply from support. This is anecdotal and may be account-specific. — [Apple Dev Forums thread 842061](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/842061)
- Apple payment requirements: a Paid Apps Agreement, banking info and a US tax form (W-8BEN for non-US), all done by the account holder. Some regions need extra documentation. Payment comes within 45 days of fiscal month-end, subject to a minimum threshold. — [Apple: View payments and proceeds](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/getting-paid/view-payments-and-proceeds/); [Apple: Enter banking info](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-banking-information/enter-banking-information/); [Apple: Provide tax info](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-tax-information/provide-tax-information/)
- Google Play: Serbia was announced as a new seller country, so Serbian developers can charge in RSD. A 2018 Play Console update added RSD local pricing (RSD 99–41,000). A merchant/payments profile is required to sell; without one, apps can only be free. — [Supreme Factory](https://supremefactory.net/ecosystem/serbia-is-a-new-seller-country-in-google-play-thanks-to-serbian-entrepreneurs/); [Android Police 2018](https://www.androidpolice.com/2018/11/30/paid-apps-on-the-play-store-are-available-in-georgia-and-myanmar-local-currency-supported-in-paraguay-and-serbia/); [Play Console supported locations](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9306917?hl=en); [Create a payments profile](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/7161426?hl=en)
- Apple Small Business Program: 15% commission if proceeds (after commission) were under $1M in the prior calendar year, or for new developers. Associated accounts count together. Crossing $1M brings back 30% for the rest of the year, with requalification the year after dropping below. The reduced rate starts after approval, so you must apply. Eligibility is calculated in USD. — [Qonversion](https://qonversion.io/blog/apple-reduces-app-store-commission-to-15); [Appbot](https://appbot.co/blog/app-developers-apple-google-small-business-programs/); [builtbyfoundry](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/apple-small-business-program-15-percent-creators)
- Google Play's equivalent: 15% on the first $1M of annual earnings (Appbot covers both programs). — [Appbot](https://appbot.co/blog/app-developers-apple-google-small-business-programs/)

### Inferences
- The practical path is to enroll (possibly as a sole proprietor or "preduzetnik" or a company with a D-U-N-S number if individual enrollment errors persist), sign the Paid Apps Agreement, file the W-8BEN and add a Serbian bank account that accepts USD or EUR SWIFT transfers.
- Serbian income-tax and VAT treatment of App Store proceeds needs a local accountant (not researched).

- No official Apple list confirming Serbian banks are supported for payouts was retrieved.
- Local tax obligations were not covered.
