# Alternative "Camera -> AI Result -> Paywall" Niches (beyond skin & calories), as of Oct 2026

Scope note: about 16 searches/fetches. Appfigures pages could not be fetched (DNS failure), so Appfigures figures come from search snippets. Most per-app revenue numbers are third-party *modeled estimates* (AppCurrents, AppRank, screensdesign) or founder claims. Only TrustMRR/RevenueCat entries are payment-verified. Older data is flagged inline.

## 1. Market size & incumbents per niche (top apps, ratings, pricing, revenue)

### Takeaway
Of all "identifier" apps, plant ID takes the most money (~78% of ~$27M/month in May 2025), but one incumbent (Glority's PictureThis) owns it. Coin and rock ID are the only other $1M+/month ID segments, and studio-made clone apps fill them. Outside pure identification, the "judge me / style me" niches (outfit rating, AI stylist, hairstyle, looksmaxxing) show strong revenue for recent entrants, including 2025-launched apps.

### Cited Findings
**Identifier apps overall (Appfigures, May 2025, gross before store fees, ~17 months old)**
- Appfigures counted 1,328 identifier apps across iOS and Google Play. Consumer spend in the month was about $27M. [Appfigures insight via search snippet](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20250509?f=1)
- Plant identifiers: 23% of apps, **78% of revenue**. Coin identifiers: about 9% of apps, 13% of revenue (~$3.5M/month). Rock/mineral: the only other group above $1M/month (~4%). Birds, bugs and mushrooms each fall under $1M/month and pass $1M only when added together. [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20250509?f=1); [This Week in Apps, "Silent Millionaires"](https://appfigures.com/resources/this-week-in-apps/20250509)
- An AppBrain top-grossing US Google Play Tools list (Aug 2025) included several coin identifiers (CoinSnap, CoinIn). [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/stats/google-play-rankings/top_grossing/tools/us)

- One aggregator puts PictureThis at 4.7 stars from ~1.8M reviews, with "polarized" sentiment about pricing. [Unstar 2026](https://unstar.app/blog/plant-identification-apps-ranked-picturethis-plantin-plantnet-2026)

### Inferences
- Plant ID has the most money but the most lock-in (PictureThis's 1.8M reviews). Coin and rock are cash-rich but crowded with Next Vision and clone studios. A new solo app there would compete on paid ads against studios with better economics.
- The "judge my appearance" cluster (outfit, hair, looksmax) is where 2025–26 entrants (Stylio, Aesty, Hair AI) show real revenue. That suggests demand isn't locked up, at least in sub-niches.
- Homework belongs to ByteDance, Zuoyebang and Google, so it should be avoided. Pet-health and car-damage consumer apps show weak monetization evidence.

## 3. User complaints a modern vision LLM could fix

### Takeaway
Complaints about incumbent ID apps are mostly about billing and dark-pattern trials. Accuracy complaints are about missing correction flows and single-answer results, which a conversational vision LLM can address with ranked candidates, follow-up questions and plain-language care advice.

### Cited Findings
- PictureThis: frequent complaints about unclear trials, unexpected charges and failed cancellations. "Free trials that auto-charge $30 after 3 days with no warning." [Common Sense Media reviews](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/picturethis-plant-identifier/user-reviews/adult); [Unstar 2026](https://unstar.app/blog/plant-identification-apps-ranked-picturethis-plantin-plantnet-2026); [Kimola report](https://kimola.com/reports/insightful-user-feedback-report-on-picturethis-app-google-play-150977)
- PictureThis misidentified 5 of 6 known plants in one Dec 2024 review, "no way to identify that it was a mistake... or re-search other options." [Holistic Management review](https://www.holisticmanagement.org/post/app-review-picturethis)
- Dressly reviews: billing and cancellation complaints, $11.99/week pricing. [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/dressly-outfit-ai-stylist/world.dressly.fashion)
- Style DNA has a 3.12 rating, suggesting the older-generation stylist UX disappoints. [AppCurrents](https://appcurrents.com/apps/style-dna-fashion-ai-stylist-android-style.dna.app)

### Inferences
- "Honest pricing" plus "multi-candidate answer with reasoning and follow-up chat" is a credible differentiator against weekly-subscription clones.
- For coins and antiques, an LLM can explain *why* something is valuable (mint mark, error type), but valuation accuracy needs price data that a model can't supply alone. That makes it a data moat problem, not an AI problem.

### Gaps
- No systematic review-mining for coin, rock, Yuka, hair or looksmax apps was done within the time budget.

## 4. Virality fit for faceless TikTok/Shorts content

### Takeaway
(Mostly inference. Little direct sourcing was found.) Results that score or rate the user (outfit 1–10, looksmax score) and before/after visuals (hair, room) fit short-form video best. "Is this coin worth $X?" reveals are also proven content formats.

### Cited Findings
- Umax grew to 800k+ downloads/month within ~2 months of launch (early 2024), widely attributed to TikTok rating content. [Baijing](https://www.baijing.cn/article/47863)
- Coin identifier apps appear in top-grossing Tools charts, which fits "pocket change worth thousands" content. [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/stats/google-play-rankings/top_grossing/tools/us)

### Inferences
- Faceless-friendly: coin/rock reveals, room before/after, outfit-on-hanger or mirror shots with a score overlay. Face-required: looksmax and hair try-on, which are harder to do faceless and carry more reputational risk.
- Plant ID content is less "wow" and its main hook is utility ("why is my plant dying").

### Gaps
- No TikTok view/hashtag data was collected.

## 5. Risks: regulation, inference cost, App Store 4.3 spam

### Takeaway
Image-*generation* niches (room redesign, hairstyle, tattoo, outfit try-on) cost far more per result than vision-*analysis* niches (ID, rating, scanners). Health-adjacent niches (pet health, hair loss, allergies, mushroom edibility) carry claims and liability risk. Saturated ID categories are the prime target for Guideline 4.3(a) rejections.

### Cited Findings
- Apple's 4.3(a) rejection notice targets apps "very similar to other developers' submissions in binary, metadata, or concept," including repackaged templates. [Apple Dev Forums 812655](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/812655); [775805](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/775805)
- Anecdotal reports say 4.3 is applied more to AI-built apps in 2025–26. The fix is differentiation (named audience, proprietary data, distinct workflow). Thin AI wrappers are often rejected under 4.2 instead. [ptkd](https://ptkd.com/journal/app-store-rejection-4-3-a-ai-slop-design-spam); [vp0](https://vp0.com/blogs/apple-app-store-ai-approval-flutterflow-cursor)
- Looksmaxxing apps are under press scrutiny for harm to teen mental health. [texxr](https://texxr.com/868607/looksmaxxing-apps-worsen-teen-mental-health)
- Interior and room redesign pricing ($19–$119/month, credit packs) reflects generation cost. [roomcalm](https://roomcalm.com/blog/best-ai-room-design-tools); [genroom](https://genroom.io/blog/best-ai-interior-design-tools)

### Inferences
- Mushroom ID: edibility misidentification is a real safety risk, so avoid edibility claims.
- Hair loss tracking: stay on "cosmetic tracking" claims and avoid diagnosis (FDA/medical-device territory).
- Pet health: vet-advice liability, plus weak monetization evidence (~$7 MRR example).
- Coin and rock: the main risk is 4.3 because dozens of clones exist, so a distinct concept is needed (e.g. collection portfolio with value tracking, specialization by country/era).

### Gaps
- No quantified per-image inference costs were sourced in this pass. Vision-LLM analysis is generally cents or less per image, while image generation costs more, but this is unsourced here.
