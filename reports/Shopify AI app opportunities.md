# Build the catalog fixer Shopify left behind

The best-supported bet for a solo developer in Serbia is a **Shopify App Store app, not a standalone Claude/ChatGPT connector**: specifically an AI "catalog fixer" that diagnoses and repairs Google Merchant Center disapprovals and missing product attributes (GTINs, taxonomy, metafields) for stores with hundreds to tens of thousands of SKUs, priced by catalog size at roughly $29–$199 per month. It beats the original idea, a generic bulk AI description writer, which now sits in a $5–$15/month commodity tier that Shopify Magic, Sidekick and the SEO suites are absorbing. Shopify's billing also solves the problem a Serbian developer can't easily solve alone: Stripe is unavailable in Serbia, but Shopify bills the merchant and pays the partner through Hyperwallet, with **0% revenue share on the first $1M of lifetime revenue** plus a 2.9% processing fee. The money is less comfortable than the product. Documented solo ramps run **3–6 months under $500 MRR and $1k–3k MRR within the first year**, so $20k/month "within a few months" is very unlikely under any option in this report; 12–24 months is a realistic target, and only if the app works. There is a second constraint the user may not have priced in: **paušal taxation caps annual income at 6,000,000 RSD (roughly €51k), far below $240k a year**, so success at the target level forces a move to bookkeeping-based taxation or a d.o.o. Nearly every rating, review count and price below comes from search-engine snippets, because the research environment couldn't load apps.shopify.com, shopify.dev or most vendor pages. Treat every number as provisional until you've checked it on the live listing.

## Weak incumbents are mostly Shopify's own free apps, and the real rivals are rated 4.7★ or higher

The pattern in the review data is consistent: **Shopify's free first-party apps collect hundreds of angry reviews, while third-party alternatives in the same categories hold 4.7–5.0★**. GapQuery's August 2026 screen counts 13 apps with 100+ reviews rated under 3.5★, against an ecosystem average of 4.45★, and several of the worst are Shopify's own: Bundles (2.70), Order Printer (3.20), Search & Discovery (3.40) and Subscriptions (3.50) ([GapQuery](https://www.gapquery.com/blog/disruption-targets-shopify)). Shopify Retail Barcode Labels sits at about **2.2–2.3★ across ~560 reviews** ([Meetanshi](https://meetanshi.com/blog/shopify-app-store-statistics/); [MageComp](https://magecomp.com/blog/shopify-app-store-statistics/)). The lesson is easy to miss. A weak free app proves demand and willingness to switch, but you are competing against the strong third-party apps merchants already switch to.

## Bulk AI descriptions are a crowded commodity, but catalog data quality is not

As literally stated, a "bulk AI product content app for large catalogs" is **weakly validated and high-risk**. Entry pricing has collapsed. Power Bulk tops out at $29.99/month ([App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/powerbulk)), Avada charges $14.90, Kraft AI charges $3.99–$7.99, and Profitonium shows 4.9★ from ~397 reviews with plans from $14.90 ([fudge.ai](https://www.fudge.ai/blog/best-shopify-ai-content-apps/); [App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/automated-description-writing)). SEO suites give the same features away inside apps merchants already pay for. TinyIMG (~5.0★, ~2,400 reviews, $14–$49) and Booster (4.8★, ~4,276 reviews) bundle AI alt text and meta tags ([charleagency](https://www.charleagency.com/articles/best-seo-apps-shopify/)). New entrants keep coming: "Sideklick" launched on 1 September 2026 with bulk SEO rewrites at $29/month ([App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/fellow-chatbot-assistant)).

Shopify itself is moving in. Magic is free on all paid plans, and the Winter '26 Edition repositioned Sidekick as a "proactive collaborator" that makes changes with merchant oversight ([Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/news/winter-26-edition-merchant)). Secondary sources describe Sidekick doing **bulk edits of SEO fields across a collection, with each change shown for approval** ([eseospace](https://eseospace.com/blog/shopify-sidekick-and-magic/)), although no official changelog confirmed this or its limits. Translate & Adapt reportedly raised free auto-translation from 2 to 8 languages in October 2026 for eligible stores ([creatorstoolbox](https://creatorstoolbox.com/blog/shopify-translate-and-adapt-eu-update-auto-sync-translations-for-up-to-8)). That is unverified, but it is the direction to expect. Assume any feature that is just "rewrite fields in bulk" becomes native within 12–18 months.

The opening that survives is narrower and more technical. Agentic Storefronts now **auto-syndicate eligible products to ChatGPT, Copilot, Google AI Mode and Gemini at no charge** ([digitalapplied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/shopify-sell-inside-chatgpt-ai-mode-copilot-guide)). That makes Shopify the pipe and catalog *data* the bottleneck. Vendors claim that missing GTINs are the most common reason products fail to index in AI-shopping feeds ([genlook](https://genlook.app/blog/shopify-chatgpt-direct-shopping-how-to-prepare)). The recurring complaints in the category are about trust, not generation: bulk runs that broke formatting ([appnavigator](https://appnavigator.io/app/shopicraft/reviews/)), erased product specs ([appnavigator](https://appnavigator.io/app/monkey)) or produced repetitive "stunning, versatile, premium" copy ([eesel.ai](https://eesel.ai/blog/hypotenuse-ai-review)). A wave of GEO apps already targets AI-shopping visibility, including Fourty IQ from $49/month ([App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/fourty-ai)), AgentSpex at $9.99 ([App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/agentspex)) and a GEO/UCP optimizer with rollback ([App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/geo-aeo-ucp-agentic-ready)). Their weakness is that "AI visibility" has **no independent evidence of measurable sales lift**. Google Merchant Center disapprovals give a fixer something the GEO apps can't show: a countable before-and-after.

The pricing arithmetic confirms the pivot. $20k MRR takes roughly 2,000 stores at $10, 670 at $30 or 200 at $99. No AI-description app with public MRR was found, and small AI SEO apps sit at 40–60 installs ([storecensus](https://www.storecensus.com/shopify-stores-using/seo-get-clicked)). The only plausible route is **150–400 mid-catalog merchants paying $50–$150/month**.

## Five alternatives ranked by solo fit and revenue ceiling

The research supports five non-commodity ideas, and each trades build effort against ARPU in a different way. The **AI invoice and packing-slip builder** targets Order Printer's 3.2★ rating and its core complaint that customization requires HTML/Liquid ([GapQuery](https://www.gapquery.com/blog/disruption-targets-shopify)). An LLM that turns a plain-language description or an uploaded sample PDF into a template is the cleanest "AI fixes this complaint" match in the data. Document Management also shows above-average demand at **126 reviews per app across 255 apps** ([GapQuery stats](https://www.gapquery.com/shopify-app-store-statistics)). It is the easiest to build and support, but ARPU is likely low and Sufio's strength wasn't measured.

The **AI bundle builder** has the strongest monetization proof. WideBundle reported **$25k MRR within a year** (in 2021) ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-bootstrapped-a-shopify-app-to-25k-mrr-in-less-than-a-year-ama-89f3d4c471)), and the Kaching portfolio reportedly makes six-figure MRR ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/getting-out-of-the-freelancing-game-by-building-a-100k-mrr-shopify-app-portfolio-qdReVAgLjz6EpW4OrJSI)). That success is exactly why the category is now full of indie-built rivals, and bundles touch checkout and inventory, which raises support load.

The **flat-priced AI support agent** attacks Gorgias double-billing AI resolutions. It has the most demand and the worst solo fit: merchants depend on it for their own customers, and LLM costs vary. The **AI translation add-on** (text inside images, glossary enforcement) fills real gaps in Translate & Adapt ([App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/translate-and-adapt/reviews)), but Shopify's reported free expansion to 8 languages undercuts it. The fifth idea, the **Merchant Center catalog fixer**, is the recommendation below. One more data point matters: Zigpoll, a solo founder in post-purchase surveys, self-reports **$125k MRR after about two years**, with the App Store bringing about a third of signups ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/hitting-125k-mrr-as-a-solo-founder-by-doubling-down-on-the-right-segment-c4o2Tfs6mjdpip5yZhaO)). Narrow, data-oriented tools can scale solo, but the timescale is years.

## Shopify's payout rails probably work from Serbia, but paušal will not survive success

Shopify's economics are excellent for a small developer. It sunset the annual reset and now exempts the **first $1M of lifetime revenue** (counted from 1 January 2025) from revenue share, then takes 15%, with earnings aggregated per partner account ([Shopify changelog](https://shopify.dev/changelog/update-to-shopifys-app-developer-revenue-share)). There is a one-time $19 registration fee ([Shopify docs](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share)) and a **2.9% processing fee on Public App sales** ([Partner Program Agreement](https://www.shopify.com/partners/terms)). Whether the 2.9% applies inside the 0% tier was not confirmed. Billing must go through the Billing API or Managed Pricing, so all revenue flows through Shopify ([App Store requirements](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/shopify-app-store/app-store-requirements)). That makes the payout question make-or-break.

Payouts run through **Hyperwallet via PayPal, bank account or wire**, with availability depending on country and currency, and **Payoneer and other virtual bank accounts are explicitly not supported** ([Shopify Help Center](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/manage-account/manage-payouts-invoices/payout-method)). Payouts arrive twice a month, five business days after the 15th and after month-end ([Shopify Help Center](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/getting-started/getting-paid?itcat=partner_blog&amp;amp;amp=&amp;amp;itterm=payout_methods)). Hyperwallet's documentation lists Serbia **without local bank deposit but with a wire option** ([Hyperwallet docs](https://docs.hyperwallet.com/content/transfer-methods/v1/bank-account)), and a third-party Hyperwallet table shows Serbian wires in USD/EUR at about a **2% fee** ([WorkMarket](https://workmarket.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/31431909954455-Hyperwallet-Supported-Countries-and-Transfer-Fees)). The likely route is a USD or EUR wire to your devizni business account. PayPal is a weak fallback, because Serbian accounts can withdraw only to a Visa card, with about 4% conversion losses ([Feruvi](https://feruvi.rs/blog/paypal-srbija)). No Serbian developer testimonial about receiving Shopify payouts was found, positive or negative. That is the single most important unknown, and a free Partner account resolves it.

US tax forms are probably benign but unconfirmed. Non-US individuals file W-8BEN to avoid default 30% withholding ([Deel](https://www.deel.com/glossary/form-w-8ben/)). There is **no US–Serbia tax treaty**, so no reduced rate exists as a fallback ([BrightTax](https://brighttax.com/countries/serbia/)). Income paid by Shopify for software built in Serbia should generally not be US-source.

The Serbian side holds the real surprises. Paušal requires total annual income, **foreign income included, of no more than 6,000,000 RSD** ([Aktiva sistem](https://aktivasistem.com/news/porez-freelancer-prihod-iz-inostranstva/)). A requested increase to 8M is tied to EU accession and not enacted ([biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/privrednici-traze-povecanje-limita-za-pausalno-oporezivanje-na-osam-miliona-dinara/)). That ceiling equals roughly €51k a year, about a fifth of the $240k target, so **paušal can carry the first year, not the goal**. The VAT threshold is 8M RSD of turnover ([biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/preduzetnik/porezi/pausalcima-se-nece-menjati-granica-za-ulazak-u-pdv/)), and one source says foreign-client turnover doesn't count toward it ([biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/sta-sve-ne-ulazi-u-godisnji-limit-za-pausalce/)). Verify that against the VAT law. Three issues need an accountant before launch. First, whether activity code 62.01 covers selling packaged software, since sources conflict ([epreduzetnik](https://epreduzetnik.rs/sifra_delatnosti/informisanje-i-komunikacije/racunarsko-programiranje-konsultantske-i-s-tim-povezane-delatnosti/racunarsko-programiranje-konsultantske-i-s-tim-povezane-delatnosti/racunarsko-programiranje/)). Second, the **independence test**, where 70%+ of income from one client is a failing criterion and Shopify is technically the single payer ([bandd.abit.rs](https://bandd.abit.rs/najcesca-pitanja-novih-pausalaca-evo-odgovora/)). Third, possible **20% reverse-charge VAT on Shopify's own fees**, which is my inference and has no source confirming it for paušalci.

## Claude and ChatGPT connectors are a channel, not a business, for now

Both AI platforms now run free, reviewed directories. Anthropic opened a self-serve submission portal on 25 September 2026 for MCP connectors and plugins ([pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/18444/anthropic-portal-submit-plugins-mcp-connectors-claude)), and the Claude Marketplace broadened to **2,000+ plugins and connectors** on 23 September ([BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-turns-claude-into-an-ai-marketplace-with-2-000-plus-plugins-and-connectors)). Its payment mechanism, where enterprises spend from their Anthropic commitment, is a limited preview aimed at enterprise vendors. OpenAI opened ChatGPT app submissions on 17 December 2025 ([the-decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-launches-app-submissions-and-rolls-out-store-in-the-new-year/)) but **forbids selling subscriptions, credits or digital goods inside apps** ([Moburst](https://www.moburst.com/blog/chatgpt-app-directory-explained-what-brands-need-to-know/)). In March 2026 it abandoned Instant Checkout after a reported sub-1% conversion rate ([Digital Commerce 360](https://digitalcommerce360.com/2026/03/06/openai-shifts-checkout-plans-agentic-commerce-strategy/)). Partners report low traffic, slow approvals and almost no usage data ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/retailers-are-rushing-to-build-ai-apps-its-unclear-if-shoppers-will-use-them/)). Claude review reportedly takes from 20 days to 2 months ([Reddit snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1vfyomj/snapshots/2026-08-06T19%3A47%3A15.7352Z)).

The monetization record is thin. Industry estimates put **monetized MCP servers below 5%**, at $5–$50/month with real churn ([mcpize](https://mcpize.com/blog/make-money-with-mcp)), and no verified indie MCP revenue case surfaced. A connector business also brings back the payments problem: **Stripe is unavailable to Serbian businesses** ([incorpuk](https://incorpuk.com/blog/how-to-open-stripe-account-in-serbia/)). Polar is the only merchant of record whose docs explicitly list Serbia ([Polar docs](https://polar.sh/docs/documentation/polar-as-merchant-of-record/supported-countries)). Paddle (5% + $0.50) excludes only sanctioned jurisdictions, so it is likely usable but unconfirmed ([Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle)). Generic Shopify-to-Claude connectors are also commoditized, because Shopify ships official Storefront, Catalog, Checkout and Customer Account MCP servers ([chattergo](https://www.chattergo.com/blog/shopify-mcp-ecosystem-deep-dive-developer-ai-platform)) and analytics vendors like Polar Analytics already connect stores to Claude ([Polar Analytics](https://www.polaranalytics.com/post/shopify-mcp-server-connect-your-store-to-claude-ai)). The verdict is that connectors are **not the better path**. Treat them as a later feature of the Shopify app, billed through Shopify, and as a listing that adds trust and discovery.

## The recommendation: an AI Merchant Center and catalog fixer

**Build an embedded Shopify app that connects a store's Google Merchant Center account, pulls every disapproval and warning, then uses an LLM to propose fixes in bulk with a diff preview, staged apply and one-click rollback.** Fixes include titles, missing GTIN/brand/MPN flags, Shopify standard-taxonomy categories, metafield attributes such as material, color and size, and policy-violating copy. Add an "AI-shopping readiness" score per product as the secondary hook, and treat descriptions and alt text as features, not the product. Position it as an add-on that works *alongside* the free Google & YouTube app and Simprosys, not as a replacement feed. The target buyer is the merchant among the Google app's ~5,500 reviewers whose top 1-star complaints are sync and approval failures ([appstoreresearch](https://appstoreresearch.com/shopify-app-reviews/google)).

Four reasons put it ahead of the other ideas. It turns the user's original bulk-content idea into something with a measurable outcome: "312 products disapproved → 41". GEO apps can't show that, and Sidekick's chat-and-approve flow isn't built for it. It justifies $29–$199 pricing by catalog size, instead of $10. Agentic Storefronts make the same attribute work more valuable every quarter. And it is text-only LLM work, which is cheap to run and self-serve to support. It has three main risks. Simprosys at 4.9★ is a formidable neighbor. Shopify or Google could ship native "fix this disapproval" suggestions. And Google's API access and OAuth verification for Merchant Center scopes may add weeks; I did not research that, and it must be checked in week 0. The invoice builder is the fallback if the Google API path turns out to be blocked, because it is the next-cleanest complaint-to-AI match.

**Pricing** (flat, by SKU count, through Shopify Billing; this is my proposal, not sourced data): Free gives a full catalog scan, the readiness score and 25 fixes. **Starter is $29/month** for up to 1,000 SKUs. **Growth is $79/month** for up to 10,000 SKUs, with scheduled re-scans and brand-voice and glossary rules. **Scale is $199/month** for up to 50,000 SKUs, adding multi-market feeds and priority text support. Annual plans are 2 months free. Fair-use caps on AI rewrites prevent the loss-leader problem seen in flat MCP pricing ([godberrystudios](https://godberrystudios.com/posts/how-to-monetize-mcp-servers-2026/)). At a blended ~$75, $20k MRR needs about **270 paying stores**.

## Conclusion

The research shifts the question from "which AI feature is missing?" to "where does AI produce a number a merchant can see change?" The commodity tier of AI copywriting is being priced to zero by cheap models and absorbed by Shopify. Durable value has moved to the boring layer underneath, structured catalog data, because Shopify has made itself the free pipe into every AI shopping surface. A disapproval count is a proof of value that a generated paragraph never is. That same proof is what lets a self-serve app charge mid-market prices without a sales call.

The constraints for a Serbian developer point the same way. Shopify's billing gets around Serbia's missing Stripe, while AI-platform directories offer no billing and no measurable discovery. The honest plan is therefore a 12–24-month build toward $20k/month, not a 3-month sprint, with tax structure upgraded ahead of the 6M RSD ceiling rather than after it. Everything numeric here came through search snippets and needs re-checking on live pages. The two cheapest checks, the Partner payout screen and an hour with an accountant, should happen before the first line of code.
